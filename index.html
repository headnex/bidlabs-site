// Proof

A visibility signal that showed up in registrations.

An anonymized broker case, measured through daily rank monitoring and the client’s own acquisition analytics.

Regulated broker · TradingView search visibility.

Across roughly 400 ticker searches, the broker reached #1 on about half of its priority instruments and Top 3 on roughly two thirds. TradingView referrals added approximately 1,000–1,300 registrations per month over baseline, without additional media spend.

Those users converted to funded accounts at 2–3× the broker’s blended rate, representing about $25–30k in first deposits per month versus an estimated $80–100k paid-media equivalent. Multiple pauses and restarts showed the same pattern: rankings moved first, registrations followed about two weeks later, and lead quality stayed stable.

// FAQ

Things prospects usually ask first.

What is Bidlabs Market Signals?
It is our intelligence layer for platform-controlled markets. We collect data that standard analytics tools do not show, turn it into consistent metrics and connect every signal to a practical marketing decision.
Which platforms do you cover?
Our current focus includes TradingView, Google Play, the App Store and Apple Ads. We add new ecosystems where important market, visibility or competitive data is difficult to access through standard tools.
Is this analytics or execution?
The core is measurement, interpretation and prioritization. Depending on the platform, we can also support implementation directly or provide detailed recommendations for the client’s marketing, product and partnership teams.
How do you connect visibility to registrations?
We track visibility and rank movement first, then compare those signals with referral visits, registrations and downstream conversion from the client’s own analytics. We define the attribution window and baseline before the work begins.
Will you name your clients?
Not in public materials. We will name comparable engagements on the demo call under NDA. Anonymized data points on this site are real and measured in our own instrumentation.
